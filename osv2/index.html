ReactOS, in your browser

The real ReactOS 0.4.16, the free operating system that runs Windows programs, on a PC that is emulated right here in the page. It starts at the desktop once about 45 MB has downloaded, and everything runs on your device.

Jazz Jackrabbit's shareware episode is on the desktop.

Works best on a computer with a mouse and keyboard. Prefer something quicker? LövOS is the ReactOS-style desktop that starts instantly.

Click the screen to use the mouse in ReactOS. Press Esc to get it back.

About this computer

ReactOS 0.4.16 (2026), started from its live CD, running in v86, a PC emulator in WebAssembly. Nothing leaves your browser: the machine has no network.

C: is a small hard disk with the desktop, My Documents and Jazz Jackrabbit. What you change lasts until you close or reload the page; Save keeps the whole machine in this browser so you can resume it later (about 50 MB of storage).

Jazz Jackrabbit

ReactOS can't run this DOS game yet: its DOS support takes the game's protected-mode program for a 16-bit Windows one. So the icon on the desktop asks this page to open it in DOSBox (LövOS's MS-DOS window), beside ReactOS, at full speed. The game is installed and saved in LövOS's drive, so it's the same game in both places.

Jazz Jackrabbit is shareware by Epic MegaGames, Inc., who own it. Shareware is try-before-you-buy software: Epic let anyone share the first episode for free, unaltered. This is that original release, unchanged.
