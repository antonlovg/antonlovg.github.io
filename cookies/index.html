Cookie policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Short version: this site sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. That's why there's no cookie banner. The game library and the LövOS desktop keep your own data in your browser so they work, and that's all.

Cookies

lovgren.dev does not set any cookies, first-party or third-party.

Local storage

The site uses your browser's local storage to remember your theme and language choices, and the game library uses it for your library and settings. This is strictly necessary for features you use yourself, so no consent is needed under EU ePrivacy rules. Nothing in it is sent to me or anyone else. It stays until you delete it.

All pages

NamePurpose
lovgren-themeYour theme choice (Light, Dark, Ocean or Ember). Only saved if you pick one; “System” saves nothing.
lovgren-langYour language (English, Svenska or Norsk). Only saved when you pick a language or answer the language suggestion.
lovgren-weatherSet to “off” if you turn off weather effects in the theme menu. Nothing is saved while they’re on.

Game library (/games/)

NamePurpose
personal-game-library-v1The games in your library
personal-game-library-preferences-v1Sort order and which shared library version you last loaded (older versions also kept the theme here)
library-backup-tip-dismissedHides the backup tip after you close it
personal-game-library-github-tokenOnly for the site owner, and only if they choose “Remember on this device” when publishing. Never set for visitors.

LövOS desktop (/os/)

LövOS keeps its C: drive in your browser's IndexedDB storage rather than local storage, and saves a copy of its own program files so it also works offline. Nothing in it is sent anywhere unless you turn on GitHub Sync.

NamePurpose
lovgren-osYour LövOS files, installed games, save games and settings. If you set up GitHub Sync, also your repository details and access token.
lovos-v1The offline copy of LövOS's own program files (not your files)
/os/sw.jsA service worker that serves that offline copy when there's no connection. It only handles LövOS.

Data from older versions

Earlier versions of the site saved notepad-content, theme and playerStats (old homepage) and game-library-data, game-library-collections, game-library-active-list, game-library-theme, game-library-view and game-library-sort (old game library). The current site doesn't read or write them. If you visited before, you can remove them by clearing this site's data.

Third-party content

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Two kinds of content come from other services:

  • YouTube trailers load only after you select “Load YouTube video”. They use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), but YouTube may still store data in your browser while the video plays.
  • Cover pictures on the homepage and in the game library, and Steam trailers when you play one, come from Steam's servers. They don't set cookies on this site.

See the privacy policy for details.

How to delete it

Open your browser's settings for this site (usually the icon to the left of the address bar) and choose to clear its data or cookies. In the game library you can export a backup first.
