Cookie policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Short version: this site sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. That's why there's no cookie banner. The game library keeps your own data in your browser so it works, and that's all.

Cookies

lovgren.dev does not set any cookies, first-party or third-party.

Local storage

The game library uses your browser's local storage to remember your library and settings. This is strictly necessary for features you use yourself, so no consent is needed under EU ePrivacy rules. Nothing in it is sent to me or anyone else. It stays until you delete it.

Game library (/games/)

personal-game-library-github-tokenOnly for the site owner, and only if they choose “Remember on this device” when publishing. Never set for visitors.

Data from older versions

Earlier versions of the site saved notepad-content, theme and playerStats (old homepage) and game-library-data, game-library-collections, game-library-active-list, game-library-theme, game-library-view and game-library-sort (old game library). The current site doesn't read or write them. If you visited before, you can remove them by clearing this site's data.

Third-party content

The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Two kinds of content come from other services:

  • YouTube trailers load only after you select “Load YouTube video”. They use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), but YouTube may still store data in your browser while the video plays.
  • Cover pictures on the homepage and in the game library, and Steam trailers when you play one, come from Steam's servers. They don't set cookies on this site.

See the privacy policy for details.

How to delete it

Open your browser's settings for this site (usually the icon to the left of the address bar) and choose to clear its data or cookies. In the game library you can export a backup first.
