Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Short version: this site has no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no cookies. I don't collect personal data. Anything you add to the game library stays in your own browser unless you choose to send it somewhere.

Who is responsible

This website, lovgren.dev, is a personal site run by Anton Lövgren. For any privacy question or request, email anton@lovgren.dev.

What I collect

Nothing automatically. The site has no contact forms, no accounts, no newsletter and no analytics. I don't log or see who visits.

If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply, I don't share it, and I delete the conversation when it's no longer needed. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering messages sent to me (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and may log them for security and to keep the service running. I don't have access to those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Data stored in your browser

The game library saves your games and settings in your browser's local storage so they're still there next time, and if you pick a theme or a language, the site remembers that choice the same way. The LövOS desktop keeps its files, games and settings in your browser's IndexedDB storage, and the ReactOS page (/osv2/) keeps a session there too, but only when you choose Save. This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself or turn on GitHub Sync in LövOS, and you can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. The cookie policy lists every item that may be stored.

Requests to other services

This page, the cookie policy, the World Cup page and the ops, status and changelog pages, in every language, load nothing from other servers, and fonts are hosted on this site. The homepage loads only the game cover pictures in “Now playing” from Steam's image servers. An automated check blocks any update that would make these pages contact any other server. A few features in the game library also contact other services. Each of the services below receives your IP address when it's used:

ServiceWhenWhat it receives
Steam image servers (Valve) When the homepage's “Now playing” section or a game card in the game library shows a cover picture A request for that image. No referrer is sent.
Steam store Only when you select an “Open Steam” link, which opens Steam in a new tab Whatever you do on Steam’s own site
Cloudflare (Steam lookup helper) Only when you select “Fetch Steam info” or “Fetch trailers” The numeric Steam app ID. The helper passes only that ID on to Steam.
Steam video servers Only when you play a Steam trailer The video stream request
Steam store prices Never from your browser: the site’s build fetches prices in euro and Norwegian kroner once per deploy Nothing about you
Weather data from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0) Never from your browser: the site’s build fetches the current weather in Stockholm about once an hour Nothing about you
YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) Only after you choose to load a YouTube trailer The video request; YouTube may store data in your browser while it plays
GitHub API (api.github.com) Only in the LövOS desktop (/os/), and only if you turn on GitHub Sync with your own access token Your token and the files on your LövOS drive, which are stored in your own GitHub repository

Each of these services handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Uptime monitoring

An automated job checks once an hour that the site and its Steam lookup helper respond, and publishes the results on the status page. It only measures the site itself and collects nothing about visitors.

Sharing and transfers

I don't sell, rent or share personal data. Because GitHub and the services above are global companies, data they receive may be processed outside the EU/EEA under their own safeguards.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data I hold about you (in practice, only emails you've sent me), ask me to correct or delete it, and object to how it's used. Email anton@lovgren.dev. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, IMY, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top.
